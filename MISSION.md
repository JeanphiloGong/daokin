# Mission (Long-term Vision)

This file captures the long-term mission and philosophical direction of DaoKin.
The project README stays practical and MVP-focused; this document is the place for the bigger “why”.

---

# DaoKin（道友）

**DaoKin — Where kin become Daos.**  
**同道者聚，诸道自生。**

DaoKin is an exploration of a decentralized community network.
It is built on a simple belief: artifacts and contributions should belong to the people who create them, and communities should be able to govern themselves without being captured by centralized platforms or external forces.

## Vision
- Help people find their place.
- Make contribution produce real value.
- Let communities arise naturally and evolve through self-governance.
- Keep the center empty so the network can outlive any single operator.

## Core Ideas
### 1) Data belongs to individuals
People own their identity, artifacts, and relationships. The default posture is user ownership, not platform custody.

### 2) Value flows to contributors
Using, citing, or obtaining value from someone else's work should require fair exchange.
Value returns to the people who create and maintain the commons.

### 3) Communities arise naturally
Circles are not assigned top-down. They form bottom-up around shared purpose, work, and contribution.

## The Dao System
DaoKin uses a simple naming and organizational model:
- **The Dao**: the overall network.
- **The Dao of ...**: a circle (community) organized around a domain or purpose.

Examples:
- The Dao of Physics
- The Dao of Mind
- The Dao of Creation
- The Dao of Truth

Each “Dao” can be governed as a DAO over time:
> Every Dao is a DAO — a decentralized civilization cell.

## Identity
In DaoKin, a person is not “a profile for engagement”.
A person is a sovereign participant and a builder of communities.

Connections should come from shared paths, not from manipulative recommendation loops.

## Artifacts
Artifacts are not just content; they are proofs of contribution.
- Artifacts belong to creators.
- Artifacts can generate value through permissioned use.
- Artifacts accumulate reputation and trust.

## Value System
The value system is a coordination tool, not a speculation engine:
- artifact permissions and revenue splits
- circle public funds
- grants and support for contributors
- settlement for governance and collaboration

Principle:
> To receive value, one must pay a fair price; contribution must be acknowledged.

## Manifesto
DaoKin is not a platform.
It is a place where communities can grow without a center.

Artifacts belong to individuals.
Value flows to contributors.
Daos arise from kinship, not control.

**DaoKin — Where kin become Daos.**  
**道友 — 同道者聚，诸道自生。**
